Hi I am Rose! I am currently a full time computer science student and a hobbist game designer when I can find the time.

I have done all different varieties of programming, but my favorite types of projects to make are ones designed to be interacted with.

Be it video games where I am trying to invoke a certain feeling through mechanical interactions, or be it making tools designed not only to work but to feel good using, either way I am fascinated by how we interact with software and how to make those experience enjoyable.

PROJECTS:

DREAMS-OF-DISQUIET

Dreams of Disquiet is the first full game I have created and published. It took me and my collaborator Steve 6 months to make, and over that time I have learned a lot about designing and implementing game mechanics, creating tools, and writing stories. As well as how to handle the full production process from pre-production to post-mortem.

[CATS+]-INTERACTIVE-CINEMA

In 2023 I got my first ever official gig making art. It was an artist residency program at MoHa (Museum of Human Achievement) In which I was given a stipend and tasked with making an interactive project that could be displayed at MoHa’s drive-in theatre. Me and a team of 2 other artists made an interactive Karaoke night with a set full of props that people could interact with in order to change the visuals on the drive in screen.

WEIGHTED-ARRAY-PACKAGE

A unity package I made to add an easy way to create weighted tables for randomized selection that can be easily edited in the unity inspector.
